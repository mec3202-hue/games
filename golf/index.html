⛳ 9 Card Golf

New game

Opponents
How to play
  • Everyone gets 9 cards face down in a 3×3 grid, then flips any 2 of them face up.
  • On your turn, either draw from the deck or take the top discard — then you must swap it with one of your cards (face up or face down). The first player's opening card is drawn from the deck for them.
  • The game ends as soon as someone has all 9 cards face up. Everyone else flips theirs, and the lowest score wins.
  • Points: A = 1, 2–10 = face value, J/Q = 10, K = 0, Joker = −4.
  • Three matching cards in a row or column are cleared (worth 0) — once per game.
