⛳ 9 Card Golf

New game

Opponents
How to play
  • Everyone gets 9 cards face down in a 3×3 grid. Lowest score after 9 holes wins.
  • On your turn, either draw from the deck or take the top discard.
  • Drawn from the deck: swap it with any of your cards, or throw it away and flip one of your face-down cards.
  • Taken from the discard: you must swap it with one of your cards.
  • The hole ends as soon as someone has all 9 cards face up. Everyone else flips theirs.
  • Points: A = 1, 2–10 = face value, J/Q = 10, K = 0, Joker = −4.
  • Three matching cards in a row or column are cleared (worth 0) — once per hole.
  • Whoever scored the most on a hole goes first on the next.
